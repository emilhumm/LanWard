Try it

Home Assistant (configuration.yaml) 
rest:
  - resource: {{ dashboard_url }}/api/status
    headers:
      Authorization: Bearer <key>
    scan_interval: 60
    sensor:
      - name: LanWard
        value_template: "{{ '{{' }} value_json.summary {{ '}}' }}"
        json_attributes: [level, alerts_open, findings, watched]
